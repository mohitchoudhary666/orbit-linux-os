# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in Orbit Linux a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of positive behavior include being welcoming, considerate, accepting constructive criticism, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing empathy. Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, publishing another person's private information, and other inappropriate conduct are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently exclude contributors. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers should respond promptly and fairly.
